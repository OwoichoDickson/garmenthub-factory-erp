GarmentHub — Factory ERP

User Manual · Enugu Fashion & Garment Hub

Version 1.0 · Web application

Welcome to GarmentHub, the management system for the Enugu Fashion & Garment Hub. It brings your whole factory — workers, attendance, production, inventory, sales and reports — into one place. This manual walks you through every screen, shows you what each looks like, and answers the questions people ask most.

1 · Getting started & logging in

GarmentHub runs in your web browser. Open the address your administrator gave you (for local use, that is usually http://localhost:5173). You'll see the sign-in screen.

Creating your account

  1. Click the Create account tab.
  2. Enter your full name, email, and a password (at least 6 characters).
  3. Click Create account, then sign in.
The very first account becomes the Administrator Whoever signs up first automatically gets full access. Everyone after that starts as an Office Admin until an administrator changes their role on the Users & Roles page.
Forgotten where things are? Your role decides which pages you can see — so your menu may be shorter than a colleague's. That's normal.

3 · Finding your way around

  • Sidebar — your menu. Group headings (Overview, Production, Inventory…) can be collapsed by clicking them.
  • Live shift indicator — shows the current shift (Morning / Afternoon / Night) and a running clock, so you always know which shift you're recording for.
  • Your account — bottom-left shows your name and role, with a log-out button.
  • On a phone — tap the ☰ menu icon at the top to open the sidebar.

4 · The Dashboard

Your home screen. At a glance it shows four key numbers — Total Workers, Today's Attendance, Active Production Orders, and Low Stock Materials — plus charts for attendance trend and production by department, and a recent-activity feed.

It knows the time of day The greeting and the shift line update with the current shift automatically.

5 · Workers

Your staff register. Each worker has a name, employee ID, role, department, team, shift, phone, hourly rate, status and hire date.

Add a worker

  1. Click + New Worker (top-right).
  2. Fill in the form. Name is required; the rest is optional.
  3. Click Save.

Use the search box and the Status / Department / Role dropdowns to find people. Coloured badges show status: ● active, ● on leave, ● terminated.

Attendance needs workers first You can only mark attendance for people who exist here. If the Workers list is empty, add staff (or import them) before using Attendance.

6 · Attendance

How to mark attendance

  1. Pick the date (defaults to today).
  2. Type a name in the Worker search box and select the person. A green ✓ marked tick means they already have a record for that date.
  3. The marking form appears. Choose shift, clock-in and clock-out times, and status.
  4. Hours worked and overtime (anything over 8 hours) are calculated for you.
  5. Click Mark attendance (or Update attendance if editing).
Already marked? It switches to edit. If the worker already has a record for that date and shift, the form loads their existing entry so you can correct it instead of creating a duplicate.
No “Mark attendance” button? The button only appears after you select a worker. If you can't select anyone, your Workers list is empty — add workers first.

7 · Production

Track production orders from cutting through packaging. Each order shows a progress bar (actual output ÷ target quantity) and a status: pending, in progress, completed or delayed. The four cards at the top summarise totals, completed, in-progress and delayed.

Filter by status, stage or shift; use the year and From/To date filters to narrow the list. Click the pencil to edit, the bin to delete.

8 · Weekly Tracker & PDF export

Reading the grid

Each department has a tab (Sewing, Cutting, Finishing, Ironing, Packaging/QC). For the chosen week it lists every entry with target, day output, night output, total and an efficiency % (green = on/over target, amber/red = below). The bottom row totals the week.

Moving between weeks & years

  • Use the ‹ › arrows to step one week back or forward.
  • Pick a year from the dropdown — this jumps the view to the start of that year, then step forward with › to reach any week.

Add / edit an entry

  1. Click + New Entry, choose the date, department, team/worker, product and the target/day/night numbers.
  2. Save. To change or remove an entry, use the pencil / bin icons on its row.
Export to PDF Click Export PDF to download a tidy landscape report of all entries for the selected year, grouped by month with monthly totals — ready to print or email.

9 · Hourly Tracker

For finer detail, log output hour-by-hour per worker: date, hour, shift, department, units produced and defects. Filter by date, shift or department. Useful for spotting which hours of the shift are most productive.

Layout: the same card-and-table style shown for Workers above, with columns for date, hour, worker, units and defects.

10 · Raw Materials & Finished Products

Raw Materials

Your fabric and supplies store: fabric type, colour, GSM, cost, batch number, warehouse location, supplier, quantity, unit and reorder level. Rows at or below their reorder level are highlighted, and the cards count In Stock / Low Stock / Out of Stock.

Finished Products

Goods ready to sell: SKU, name, size, colour, design code, batch, cost & selling price, quantity available and category. Filter by category or size.

Layout: the same card-and-table style shown for Workers, with columns for SKU, size, colour, category and price.

Good to know Stock quantities change only when you edit a record. Logging a Stock Movement or a Sale does not automatically adjust these numbers in this version.

11 · Stock Movements

A running log of stock activity — stock in, stock out, adjustments, production use/output and sales. Record the item, type, quantity and a reference. Think of it as your inventory diary.

Layout: the same card-and-table style as Workers, with a coloured badge for each movement type and a date column.

12 · Bin Book

Tracks small consumables (thread, zips, elastic, needles…) across Store A and Store B. Two tabs:

  • Items — the things you stock, with opening stock, current stock and reorder level. Low items are highlighted.
  • Entries — daily issuances. When you log an issuance, the item's current stock drops automatically. Delete an issuance and the stock is added back.

Log an issuance

  1. On the Entries tab click + Log Issuance.
  2. Pick the item (it shows how much you currently have), set the quantity issued and who it went to.
  3. Save — the new stock level is shown before you confirm.

13 · Sales / POS

Create customer invoices. Add line items (product, quantity, unit price) and the line totals, subtotal, discount, VAT and grand total calculate as you type. Track payment status and method.

  1. Click + New Invoice.
  2. Enter the customer and order type (wholesale / retail).
  3. Add line items with + Add item; set discount % and VAT %.
  4. Record amount paid, payment method and status, then Save invoice.
VAT note The VAT field defaults to 16%. Nigeria's standard VAT is 7.5% — change the VAT % on each invoice (or ask your administrator to set the correct default).

14 · Customers & Suppliers

Customers — wholesale and retail buyers, with contact details, credit limit and lifetime totals. Filter by type.

Suppliers — who you buy materials from, with contact person, materials supplied, payment terms and a 1–5 star rating. Filter by status.

Both use the same card-and-table layout as Workers; Suppliers shows a ★ star rating, Customers shows order and spend totals.

15 · Finances

Record expenses by category (raw materials, labour, utilities, maintenance, transport, rent, equipment, other), amount, date, payment method and who approved it. The page shows total spend, a monthly expense chart and your top category.

16 · Power Outage log

Record every power interruption. Enter the date, the time power went off and came back — the duration (e.g. “3h 33m”) is worked out automatically. Cards show this month's outages, total hours lost and the longest outage. Filter by month and year.

17 · Reports & Director's View

Reports brings production, attendance and expenses together as charts. Choose a Year, or set a From/To date range (which overrides the year), to focus the analysis.

Director's View is a read-only executive summary — production efficiency, attendance rate, orders completed, power hours lost, efficiency by department, top-performing teams and a team league table. Available to Directors and administrators.

18 · Import Center (CSV)

The Import Center (admin only) lets you bulk-add records from a spreadsheet.

  1. Find the card for the data you want (Workers, Attendance, Power Outage, etc.).
  2. Click Template to download a blank CSV with the exact column headings.
  3. Fill it in (in Excel / Google Sheets) and save as CSV — keep the headings exactly as they are.
  4. Click Upload CSV and choose your file. A progress bar runs, then a summary shows.

The summary reports four numbers: Found, Imported, Duplicates (ignored) and Skipped (rows with errors, each listed with its line number).

Safe to re-upload Duplicates are detected and ignored, so uploading the same file twice won't double your data. For example, a Worker is a duplicate if its employee_id already exists; an invoice by invoice_number; attendance by worker + date + shift.

19 · Users & Roles

Administrators manage who can do what here. It lists every user with their email, join date and current role. Use the dropdown to change someone's role; a guide at the bottom shows exactly which pages each role can see.

Two safeguards You can't change your own role (so you can't accidentally lock yourself out), and only administrators can change roles.

20 · Everyday workflows

Start-of-day (Office Admin)

  1. Open Attendance, confirm today's date.
  2. Search each worker as they clock in; set times and status; save.
  3. Check the Present / Absent / Late cards match the floor.

End-of-week production review (Supervisor)

  1. Open Weekly Tracker, make sure each department's entries are in for the week.
  2. Check the efficiency column and week totals.
  3. Click Export PDF for the records or a management meeting.

Receiving fabric (Storekeeper)

  1. Add or update the fabric in Raw Materials (quantity, batch, supplier).
  2. Log a Stock Movement of type stock in as your paper trail.

Selling to a customer (Sales)

  1. Sales / POS → + New Invoice; add the customer and line items.
  2. Set discount and the correct VAT %; record the payment.
  3. Save. Track outstanding balances with the Payment filter.

21 · Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password — what do I do?
Ask your administrator to help reset it from your Supabase account, or use the password-reset option if it has been enabled. There is no separate password screen inside GarmentHub itself.
Why can't I see some menu items my colleague has?
The menu is filtered by your role. For example, a Storekeeper sees inventory pages but not Finances. If you genuinely need access to more, ask an administrator to adjust your role on the Users & Roles page.
There's no “Mark attendance” button.
It only appears after you select a worker from the search box. If you can't select anyone, the Workers list is empty — add workers first (or import them via the Import Center).
I marked the same worker twice by mistake.
You can't, really — if a worker already has a record for that date and shift, the form switches to edit mode and updates the existing entry instead of adding a second one.
My imported records appear in a strange order.
Lists are sorted newest-first by the record's own date (e.g. attendance date, invoice date), not by when you uploaded them. So the most recent dates appear at the top regardless of import time.
I uploaded a CSV but some rows were skipped.
Open the “rows skipped or ignored” section in the import summary. Each skipped row is listed with its line number and reason — usually a missing required field (like a worker's name) or a value the database rejected. Duplicates are listed separately and are ignored on purpose.
Can I re-upload the same spreadsheet without doubling my data?
Yes. Duplicate detection means already-existing rows are ignored. The summary will show them under “Duplicates”.
Why didn't my stock go down after a sale?
In this version, Sales and Stock Movements are records/logs — they don't automatically change Raw Material or Finished Product quantities. Update those quantities directly when needed. (The Bin Book is the exception: issuing a consumable does reduce its stock automatically.)
The VAT looks too high.
The default VAT is 16%. Nigeria's standard rate is 7.5%. Change the VAT % on the invoice before saving, or ask your administrator to set the correct default.
How do I filter to a specific period?
Most list pages have a year dropdown and a From / To date filter. Set From/To for an exact range (it overrides the year). The Reports and Power Outage pages have their own period controls.
How do I export a report to share?
The Weekly Tracker has an Export PDF button that produces a printable, month-by-month report for the whole year. For other pages, use your browser's print (Cmd/Ctrl + P) and “Save as PDF”.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. The layout adapts to small screens — tap the ☰ menu icon at the top to open the sidebar.
Is my data saved automatically?
Data is saved to the secure Supabase database the moment you click Save. There's nothing extra to “save” at the end of the day.
